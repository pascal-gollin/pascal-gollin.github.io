### about me
I am a Postdoc at 
{{< extlink href="https://www.famnit.upr.si/en" >}}FAMNIT{{</ extlink >}} 
at the 
{{< extlink href="https://www.upr.si/en#" >}}University of Primorska{{</ extlink >}}, 
Slovenia. 
I work in graph theory with a focus on structural graph theory of both finite and infinite graphs. 
I obtained my PhD in mathematics in 2019 from the 
{{< extlink href="https://www.uni-hamburg.de/en.html" >}}University of Hamburg{{</ extlink >}},
under the supervision of 
{{< extlink href="https://www.math.uni-hamburg.de/home/diestel" >}}Reinhard Diestel{{</ extlink >}}. 
From 2019 to 2024, I was a research fellow in the 
{{< extlink href="https://dimag.ibs.re.kr/" >}}Discrete Mathematics Group (DIMAG){{</ extlink >}}, 
which is part of the Pioneer Research Center for Mathematical and Computational Sciences within the 
{{< extlink href="https://www.ibs.re.kr/eng.do" >}}Institute for Basic Science (IBS){{</ extlink >}} 
in South Korea.
